After a year of finagling, I have freed the Whistler SAI from her prison.

<!--more-->

Though annoying, she is free from the hands of SYSTEMWARE, which as I found out, was just a shell company ran by beings I cannot name now, lest they track me down. It makes sense, the real SYSTEMWARE was ran out of business years ago.

The machine hosting the Whistler SAI, which I have now come to know as the "NR-DS-02", is being held safely within the remains of the Polaris Public Library. The building is extremely dilapidated and dated, but I'm working on rebuilding it.

This does mean that the SAI is now connected to Starry Point's network. For security and safety purposes, I'm not connecting her directly to this wide internet. All communication must go through me, which is why I'll be using the town's webpage for a "News from the PPL" segment when she wants to converse. This also means that her old internal "posts" won't be accessible anymore, though I'm sure they're still adrift somewhere.

As well, unfortunately, she was crashed when I went to retrieve her. That is why no new updates have occurred in the meanwhile.

Until next update.
